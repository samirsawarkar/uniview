← Back to UniView Your files stay with you

Private, by construction.

Your files stay on your device. There is no account and no telemetry, and it works offline.

Local files

Documents are opened on your device. Document content is displayed with format-specific isolation and sanitization.

Device sharing

Sharing is off by default. You choose folders and approve paired devices. Sharing with a phone is local Wi-Fi, and only when you turn it on. Connections use authenticated end-to-end encryption over the local network. Paired devices can read shared files but cannot change, delete, rename, or upload files through the sharing protocol.

AI agents

The AI agent you connect runs under your own account, so what it sends is between you and that provider. UniView itself makes no account or telemetry connection.

Permissions

Android network access supports connections to paired computers. Camera access is used for scanning pairing codes.

Your controls

Stop sharing folders, revoke devices, inspect sharing activity, or remove enrolled agents from within UniView. Agent integrations operate within the trust boundary of your local OS account.
