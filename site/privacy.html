Private, by construction.

UniView renders files locally and requires no account. It does not send telemetry or upload your documents to a cloud service.

Local files

Documents are opened on your device. The desktop viewer blocks external network requests, and document content is displayed with format-specific isolation and sanitization.

Device sharing

Sharing is off by default. You choose folders and approve paired devices. Connections use authenticated end-to-end encryption over the local network. Paired devices can read shared files but cannot change, delete, rename, or upload files through the sharing protocol.

Permissions

Android network access supports connections to paired computers. Camera access is used for scanning pairing codes.

Your controls

Stop sharing folders, revoke devices, inspect sharing activity, or remove enrolled agents from within UniView. Agent integrations operate within the trust boundary of your local OS account.
