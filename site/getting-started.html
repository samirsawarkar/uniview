Connect your phone

Android: not available as a download yet. Sharing with a phone is local Wi-Fi, and only when you turn it on. The AI agent you connect runs under your own account, so what it sends is between you and that provider.

  1. Open UniView on your computer and choose Share this computer.
  2. Enable sharing and add only the folders you want to access.
  3. On the phone, choose Connect a computer and use the pairing code or QR option.
  4. Approve the device on your computer. Keep both devices on the same network.
